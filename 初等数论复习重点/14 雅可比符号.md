>[!note] Def 1 雅可比符号
>设$m=p_{1}p_{2} \cdots p_{r}$，$p_{i}$为素数，$\left(\displaystyle\frac{a}{p_{i}}\right)$是$a$对$p_{i}$的勒让德符号.
>对于给定的大于1的奇数定义在一切整数$a$上的函数:
>$$\left(\frac{a}{m}\right) = \left(\frac{a}{p_1}\right)\left(\frac{a}{p_2}\right)\cdots\left(\frac{a}{p_r}\right)$$


>[!note] Thm 1
>$$\left(\frac{a}{m}\right) =-1 \Leftrightarrow a是非平方剩余$$

注意$\displaystyle\left(\frac{a}{m}\right) =1$不能说明任何事情.

>[!note] Thm 2
>(1) $$\left(\frac{-1}{m}\right) = (-1)^{\frac{m-1}{2}}$$
>(2)$$\left(\frac{2}{m}\right) = (-1)^{\frac{m^2-1}{8}}$$
>(3)$$\left(\frac{n}{m}\right) = (-1)^{\frac{m-1}{2} \cdot \frac{n-1}{2}} \left(\frac{m}{n}\right)$$

>[!proof]
>(1)$$\left(\frac{-1}{m}\right) =\left(\displaystyle\frac{-1}{p_{1}}\right)\left(\displaystyle\frac{-1}{p_{2}}\right) \cdots\left(\displaystyle\frac{-1}{p_{r}}\right)=(-1)^{\sum_{i=1}^{r}\frac{p-1}{2}}$$
>即证明$\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{r}\frac{p-1}{2}\equiv \displaystyle\frac{p_{1}p_{2} \cdots p_{r}-1}{2} \pmod{2}$
>即证明$\displaystyle\frac{a-1}{2}+\displaystyle\frac{b-1}{2} \equiv \displaystyle\frac{ab-1}{2} \pmod{2} \Leftrightarrow a+b-2 \equiv ab-1 \pmod{4} \Leftrightarrow (a-1)(b-1) \equiv 0 \pmod{4}$
>由于$a,b$均为奇素数，所以此式成立，由归纳法，原命题成立.
>
>(2)即证明$\displaystyle\frac{a^2-1}{8}+\displaystyle\frac{b^2-1}{8} \equiv \displaystyle\frac{a^2b^2-1}{8} \pmod{2} \Leftrightarrow (a^2-1)(b^2-1) \equiv 0 \pmod{4}$
>
>(3)即证明$$\sum_{i=1}^{s} \sum_{j=1}^{t} \frac{p_i-1}{2} \cdot \frac{q_j-1}{2}\equiv \frac{p_{1}p_{2} \cdots p_{r}-1}{2} \cdot\frac{q_{1}q_{2} \cdots q_{r}-1}{2}\pmod{2}$$
>由于$\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{s} \sum_{j=1}^{t} \frac{p_i-1}{2} \cdot \frac{q_j-1}{2} = \left(\sum_{i=1}^{s} \frac{p_i-1}{2}\right)\left(\sum_{j=1}^{t} \frac{q_j-1}{2}\right)$
>再根据(1)的证明过程，上式自然成立.
