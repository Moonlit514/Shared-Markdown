>[!note] Thm 1 费马平方和定理
>素数$p \equiv 1 \pmod{4} \Leftrightarrow\left(\displaystyle\frac{-1}{p}\right)=1  \Leftrightarrow p=a^2+b^2$

>[!proof] 
>充分性.
>$a^2+b^2 \equiv 0 \pmod{p}$，则$a^2 \equiv -b^2 \pmod{ p}$，从而$\left( \displaystyle\frac{a}{b} \right)^2 \equiv -1 \pmod{p}$，从而根据前节定理，$p \equiv 1 \pmod{4}$
>必要性.
>$\left(\displaystyle\frac{-1}{p}\right)=1$，从而$a^2 \equiv -1 \pmod{p} \equiv -b^2 \pmod{p}$
>设$k= \min \{t|tp=m^2+n^2\}$且$x^2+y^2=kp$
>若$k>1$，则有$x \equiv x_{1}(k),|x_{1}|\leqslant \frac{k}{2},y \equiv y_{1}(k),|y_{1}|\leqslant \frac{k}{2}$.(见[[01 整除与带余除法#^bbda1f|01 整除与带余除法]])
>$x_{1}^2+y_{1}^2 \equiv 0 \pmod{k}$，从而$x_{1}^2+y_{1}^2 =mk$，$|m|\leqslant \frac{k}{2}$
>又由于$x_{1}^2+y_{1}^2 \equiv x^2+y^2 \pmod{k}$，得到$(x^2+y^2)(x_{1}^2+y_{1}^2)=k^2mp$
>通过转换(运用复数的模)，得到$(x x_{1}-yy_{1})^2+(y_{1}x+yx_{1})^2=k^2mp$
>得到$$\displaystyle\left(\frac{x x_{1}-yy_{1}}{k}\right)^2+\left(\frac{y_{1}x+yx_{1}}{k}\right)^2=mp$$
>又$m<k$与$k$最小矛盾，从而$k=1$.
