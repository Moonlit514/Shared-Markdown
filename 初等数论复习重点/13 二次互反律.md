>[!note] Def 1 勒让德符号
>对于一个给定的奇素数$p$，定义在一切整数的$a$上的函数：
>$$\left(\frac{a}{p}\right)=\begin{cases}1,&a\text{ 是模 }p\text{ 的平方剩余,}\\-1,&a\text{ 是模 }p\text{ 的平方非剩余,}\\0,&p\mid a.&\end{cases}$$

>[!note] Thm 1 欧拉判别条件
>$$\left(\frac{a}{p}\right) \equiv a^{\frac{p-1}{2}} \pmod{p}$$

而且由此可以得到勒让德符号的乘积可积性.
>[!proof]
>若$a$为平方剩余，即$a \equiv b^2 \pmod{p}$，则$a^{\frac{p-1}{2}} \equiv b^{p-1} \equiv 1 \pmod{p}$.
>考虑$x^{\frac{p-1}{2}}-1 \equiv 0 \pmod{p}$， 其根个数不超过$\displaystyle\frac{p-1}{2}$个，而$1^2,2^2,\dots,(\displaystyle\frac{p-1}{2})^2$已是它的$\displaystyle\frac{p-1}{2}$个根，从而$x$是平方剩余.
>
>若$a$为平方非剩余，由$a^{p-1} \equiv 1 \pmod{p}$，得到$$(a^{\frac{p-1}{2}}+1)(a^{\frac{p-1}{2}}-1)\equiv0 \pmod{p}$$
>从而$a^{\frac{p-1}{2}} \equiv -1 \pmod{p}$.
>若$a^{\frac{p-1}{2}} \equiv -1 \pmod{p}$，显然$a$不是平方剩余.


>[!note] Lemma 高斯引理
>设$(a,p)=1,\ r_{k}\equiv { ak } \pmod{p}$，且$1\leqslant r_{k}\leqslant p-1,1\leqslant k\leqslant\displaystyle\frac{p-1}{2}$.
>若$\mathrm{card}  \left\{  r_{k}|r_{k}>\displaystyle\frac{p}{2},1\leqslant k\leqslant \displaystyle\frac{p-1}{2}\right\}=m$
>则$$\left(\displaystyle\frac{a}{p}\right)=(-1)^m$$

>[!proof]
>设$a_{1},\dots,a_{t}$是一切小于$\displaystyle\frac{p}{2}$的$r_{k}$；$b_{1},\dots,b_{m}$是一切大于$\displaystyle\frac{p}{2}$的$r_{k}$，即$\left\{  r_{k}|r_{k}>\displaystyle\frac{p}{2},1\leqslant k\leqslant \displaystyle\frac{p-1}{2}\right\}=\{b_{1},\dots,b_{m}\}$.
>则$$a^{\frac{p-1}{2}}\left( \displaystyle\frac{p-1}{2} \right)! =a \cdot 1  \cdot 2 \cdots a \cdot \displaystyle\frac{p-1}{2} \equiv \prod_{i=1}^{t} a_i \prod_{j=1}^{m} b_j \pmod{p}$$
>假设$a_{i}=p-b_{j}$，则有一组$i,j$使得$a_{i}+b_{j} =p$，从而有$r_{k_{1}}+r_{k_{2}}=ak_{1}+ak_{2} \equiv a_{i}+b_{j} \equiv 0 \pmod{p}$，由于$(a,p)=1$，得到$k_{1}+k_{2} \equiv 0 \pmod{p}$. 但是$1\leqslant k\leqslant \displaystyle\frac{p-1}{2}$，所以这是不可能的.
>从而$a_{i}\neq p-b_{j}$.
>由于$b_{j} \equiv -(p-b_{j}) \pmod{p}$，且$t+m=\displaystyle\frac{p-1}{2}$，从而
>$$a^{\frac{p-1}{2}}\left(\frac{p-1}{2}\right)! \equiv (-1)^m \prod_{i=1}^{t} a_i \prod_{j=1}^{m} (p-b_j)\equiv (-1)^{m}\left( \displaystyle\frac{p-1}{2} \right)! \pmod{p}$$
>因此$$\left(\frac{a}{p}\right) \equiv a^{\frac{p-1}{2}}\equiv(-1)^{m} \pmod{p} $$

证明的思路是把所有的余数转化到$\left( 0,\displaystyle\frac{p}{2} \right)$之内，并证明这个集合就是$\left\{ 1,2,\dots,\displaystyle\frac{p-1}{2} \right\}$.
而且可以得到$$ak=p\left[ \displaystyle\frac{ak}{p} \right]+r_{k}$$


>[!note] Thm 2
>$$\left(\frac{2}{p}\right) = (-1)^{\frac{p^2-1}{8}}$$

>[!proof]
>由引理得到
>$$\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{\frac{p-1}{2}}2k=2 \cdot \displaystyle\frac{p^2-1}{8} \equiv \displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{\frac{p-1}{2}}r_{k}=\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{t}a_{i}+\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{m}b_{j}=\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{t}a_{i}+\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{m}(p-b_{j})+2\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{m}b_{j}-mp \pmod{p}$$
>即$$2 \cdot \displaystyle\frac{p^2-1}{8} \equiv\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{t}a_{i}+\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{m}(p-b_{j})+2\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{m}b_{j}-mp =\displaystyle\frac{p^2-1}{8}+2\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{m}b_{j}-mp\pmod{p}$$
>化简$$\displaystyle\frac{p^2-1}{8} \equiv 2\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{m}b_{j}-mp\equiv 2 \displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{m}b_{j}+mp\pmod{p} $$
>又$p$为奇素数，从而，$$\displaystyle\frac{p^2-1}{8} \equiv mp \equiv m\pmod{2}$$
>从而$$\left(\frac{2}{p}\right) =(-1)^{m} = (-1)^{\frac{p^2-1}{8}}$$

从而$p \equiv \pm 1 \pmod{8}$，2是平方剩余；$p \equiv \pm 3 \pmod{8}$，2是非平方剩余.


>[!note] Thm 3 二次互反律
>$$\left(\frac{q}{p}\right)\left(\frac{p}{q}\right) = (-1)^{\frac{p-1}{2} \cdot \frac{q-1}{2}}$$

>[!proof]
>$$\begin{align*} a\frac{p^2-1}{8} &= p\sum_{k=1}^{\frac{p-1}{2}} \left[\frac{ak}{p}\right] + \sum_{i=1}^{t} a_i + \sum_{j=1}^{m} b_j \\ &= p\sum_{k=1}^{\frac{p-1}{2}} \left[\frac{ak}{p}\right] + \sum_{i=1}^{t} a_i + \sum_{j=1}^{m} (p-b_j) + 2\sum_{j=1}^{m} b_j - mp \\ &= p\sum_{k=1}^{\frac{p-1}{2}} \left[\frac{ak}{p}\right] + \sum_{k=1}^{\frac{p-1}{2}} k - mp + 2\sum_{j=1}^{m} b_j \\ &= p\sum_{k=1}^{\frac{p-1}{2}} \left[\frac{ak}{p}\right] + \frac{p^2-1}{8} - mp + 2\sum_{j=1}^{m} b_j, \end{align*}$$
>从而
>$$(a-1)\frac{p^2-1}{8} = \sum_{k=1}^{\frac{p-1}{2}} \left[\frac{ak}{p}\right] + m \pmod{p}$$
>若$2 \nmid a$，则有
>$$m \equiv \sum_{k=1}^{\frac{p-1}{2}} \left[\frac{ak}{p}\right] \pmod{2}$$
>
>由此
>$$\left(\frac{q}{p}\right) = (-1)^{\sum_{k=1}^{\frac{p-1}{2}} \left[\frac{qh}{p}\right]}, \left(\frac{p}{q}\right) = (-1)^{\sum_{k=1}^{\frac{q-1}{2}} \left[\frac{pk}{q}\right]}$$
>要证$\left(\frac{q}{p}\right)\left(\frac{p}{q}\right) = (-1)^{\frac{p-1}{2} \cdot \frac{q-1}{2}}$,即证
>$$\sum_{k=1}^{\frac{p-1}{2}} \left[\frac{qh}{p}\right] + \sum_{k=1}^{\frac{q-1}{2}} \left[\frac{pk}{q}\right] = \frac{p-1}{2} \cdot \frac{q-1}{2}$$

回顾[[04 取整函数#^e551aa|04 取整函数 Exa 3]]的证明，考虑直线$y=\displaystyle\frac{p}{q}x$分割长$\displaystyle\frac{p}{2}$，宽$\displaystyle\frac{q}{2}$的矩形上半与下半的整点个数之和.
应用更多的是这个形式

>[!example] Exa 1
>判断同余式$$x^2 \equiv 286 \pmod{563}$$

判断思路：$\displaystyle\left(\frac{286}{563}\right) = \left(\frac{2}{563}\right)\left(\frac{143}{563}\right) = -\left(\frac{143}{563}\right) = -\left(\frac{11}{563}\right)\left(\frac{13}{563}\right)$$\displaystyle\left(\frac{13}{563}\right) = \left(\frac{563}{13}\right) = \left(\frac{4}{13}\right) = 1, \quad \left(\frac{11}{563}\right) = -\left(\frac{563}{11}\right) = -\left(\frac{2}{11}\right) = 1$.
